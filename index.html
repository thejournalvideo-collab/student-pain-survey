Student pain point survey

What gets in the way when you work on ideas or projects?

We're building a free app to help students turn ideas into real projects with the right people. Before we build more, we want to hear what actually gets in your way.

What you're getting into

  • About 2 minutes. Two quick questions, then you rank some problems.
  • Anonymous. We don't ask for your name, email or student number.
  • What we keep: your subject area, whether you've tried a project, your ranking and anything you type in. It's stored in a private spreadsheet.
  • How it's used: only to decide what the app should do. We may share totals (for example "40% ranked teamwork first") but never single answers.
  • Your choice. You can stop at any time and nothing is sent until you press Submit. Please don't type anything that could identify you or anyone else.
